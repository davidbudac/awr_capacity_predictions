Forecast / projection
Where a number is heading, based on its recent trend.
REGR (straight-line trend)
Fits a straight line through recent history and extends it forward.
ESM
Oracle's machine-learning forecast; it also learns weekly patterns and is usually best for the next 30 days.
95% band
The shaded wedge on a chart: the actual value should land inside it 95 times out of 100 if growth stays like the recent past.
R2
How closely growth follows a straight line: 1.00 is perfectly steady, near 0 is erratic.
Quality: OK
Steady enough to forecast reliably.
Quality: LOW_CONFIDENCE
Growth is too erratic for a dependable estimate.
Quality: FLAT
Not growing at all.
Quality: INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY
Not enough days of AWR history yet.
Anomaly
A day that stands out sharply from what is normal for that series.
Level shift
A sustained step in the level a series runs at, as opposed to a one-day outlier.
Robust z-score
How far outside its normal range a day was; 3 or more is clearly unusual.
MAD
Median absolute deviation -- a spike-resistant way of measuring "normal".
Days-to-full
Estimated days until a tablespace reaches its allocated limit.
Saturation
The busy percent at which the CPU is treated as maxed out.
